# Math Workshops: Practice and Solutions

## Overview

This directory contains a practice set based on the material covered in Workshops 1 and 2, its detailed written solutions, and a video walkthrough of the exercises.

## Files and Recording

- [Math_Workshops_Practice_Q.pdf](./Math_Workshops_Practice_Q.pdf) — Practice problems.
- [Math_Workshops_Practice_A.pdf](./Math_Workshops_Practice_A.pdf) — Detailed written solutions.
- [Practice Exercises — Video Walkthrough](https://my.files.ir/drive/s/5gUopYChzpQb4R2ZdZKsTvO65RZywJ)
## Learning Objectives

By completing the practice set, students should be able to:

- Review and apply differentiation and partial differentiation techniques.
- Use maximum and minimum conditions in optimization problems.
- Solve constrained optimization problems using substitution and the Lagrange method.
- Check their reasoning against written and video solutions.

## Instructions for Students

Attempt the problems before consulting the written solutions or watching the walkthrough. Use the solutions to identify and review any steps that were unclear.
