# Topic 01: Supply and Demand

## 📌 Overview
This recitation session introduces the market mechanism, determinants of individual supply and demand curves, and market equilibrium determination.

## 📁 Files in this Directory
- [Supply_and_Demand_Full.pdf](./Supply_and_Demand_Full.pdf) — Conceptual review slides and integrated in-class practice problems.
- [Supply_and_Demand_Solutions.pdf](./Supply_and_Demand_Solutions.pdf) — Step-by-step solutions to in-class analytical problems.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Distinguish clearly between movements along curves and shifts of curves.
- Solve for equilibrium price ($P^*$) and quantity ($Q^*$) algebraically and graphically.
- Analyze qualitative and quantitative impacts of government interventions (price ceilings and price floors).
