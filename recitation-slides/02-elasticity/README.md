# Topic 02: Elasticity

## 📌 Overview
This session covers the mathematical formulation and economic interpretation of various elasticities, including price elasticity of demand and supply, cross-price elasticity, and income elasticity.

## 📁 Files in this Directory
- [Elasticity_Full.pdf](./Elasticity_Full.pdf) — Theoretical lecture notes and integrated analytical exercises.
- [Elasticity_Solutions.pdf](./Elasticity_Solutions.pdf) — Complete solution key for elasticity derivations.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Calculate and interpret point elasticity vs. arc (midpoint) elasticity.
- Understand the relationship between price elasticity of demand and total revenue ($TR$).
- Classify goods (normal, inferior, luxury, necessity, substitutes, complements) using elasticity metrics.
